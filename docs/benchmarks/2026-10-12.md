# 生成の比較 2026-10-12（最初の記録）

同じ頼みを3つの描き手で描き、作画監督の数字（tools/gen/measure.py、--hair "#1e1a24,#a8203c"）と検品で比べる。毎週この頼みを変えずに描く。

## 頼み（毎週同じ言葉を使う）

外部の生成に渡した言葉:

```
Anime-style character illustration, full body: a female knight with black hair with crimson inner hair color, brown eyes, silver plate armor, red cape, holding one sword with both hands on the hilt, sword point resting on the ground, standing inside a cathedral with strong backlight and god rays from tall windows, rim light on hair and armor edges, clean lineart, cel shading, high detail
```

手元（Animagine）に渡した言葉:

```
1girl, solo, female knight, black hair, crimson inner hair, colored inner hair, brown eyes, silver plate armor, red cape, holding sword, both hands on sword hilt, sword point on ground, cathedral interior, full body, backlighting, rim light, <atmosphere.PROMPT["holy"]>
```
