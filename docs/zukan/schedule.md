## 画風の順（簡単なものから本格的なものへ）

1. 01-yurukyara — yuru-chara mascot, super simple round shapes, thick outline, flat colors, cute
2. 02-chibi — chibi deformed sticker, big head, flat 2d, white outline
3. 03-character — anime character design sheet style, cel shading, clean lineart
4. 04-anime — 1990s anime cel animation still
5. 05-manga — black and white manga ink drawing, screentone, monochrome
6. 06-illust — digital illustration, soft lighting, detailed
7. 07-kirie — paper cut-out art, layered colored paper, flat shapes
8. 08-nihonga — nihonga japanese painting, mineral pigments, gold leaf background
9. 09-ukiyoe — ukiyo-e woodblock print, bold outlines, flat colors
10. 10-oil — classical oil painting, visible brush strokes
11. 11-watercolor — loose watercolor painting, paper texture
12. 12-real — wildlife photograph, realistic, natural habitat, sharp focus

描いた絵は毎時28分に検品へ回し、すべて送る。検品を通った絵は画風ごとの図鑑（`docs/zukan/`）にまとめる。
